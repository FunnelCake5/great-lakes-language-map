# SPEC — Great Lakes Language Map

## Purpose
Most tellings of this history go "first it was these people, then those." This map should show what that story leaves out: two language families whose shared frontier moved in pulses over a thousand years, set in motion by forces mostly *east* of it. Inside each family the identities are fractal: a family splits into branches, then languages, dialects, nations and villages. Those identities split, merged and blended. The viewer should come away understanding that the boundaries were gradients and the units nested.

## Core thesis to make visible
1. Siouan peoples held more of the east and the south shore of Lake Michigan early on: Huber phase Chicago was ancestral Ho-Chunk, and Ohio Valley Siouan speakers lived in Virginia and the Lower Mississippi.
2. Cahokia's rise (c.1050) pulled people in; its collapse (1150–1350) and the Vacant Quarter (1450–1550) released them. The Oneota (proto-Chiwere/Ho-Chunk) filled the gap.
3. Little Ice Age drought and cold pushed the northern limit of reliable maize south (c.1300–1550).
4. From 1638 to 1701, Iroquoian warfare, armed by European trade, drove Algonquian refugees west into Siouan Wisconsin. The refugee villages had mixed populations.
5. French, British and then American systems reshaped everything again: trade posts, gifts, epidemics, treaties, cessions and removal.
6. Today the landscape still carries these languages in its place names. Missouri, for example, is a Miami–Illinois word naming a Siouan people.

## Screens and controls

### Map (main canvas)
- **Language-family fields.** A kernel-density "weather" field per family, computed from nation positions at the current year and weighted by population where known and by confidence. Where fields overlap they blend, so mixed zones show as mixed color. No hard borders.
- **Nation markers.** A glyph per nation, colored by family, labeled in small caps. They move by interpolating between position keyframes and leave a fading trail of the last 50 years. Splits animate as one marker dividing; merges animate as markers fusing.
- **Event pulses.** An event pulses at its location while the time cursor is inside its year range. Tapping one opens a card showing title, years, actors, confidence and source link.
- **Contested alternatives** render as paired ghost markers joined by a dotted line.

### Time
- Range: 900 → 1900, plus a separate **Today** state.
- **Nonlinear axis.** The years 900–1600 take 25% of the slider width, 1600–1840 take 60%, and 1840–1900 take 15%.
- Play/pause, speed control (×1/×2/×4), and step buttons that move to the previous/next event.
- **Chapters.** Named stops that match the eight Mascouten plates, plus Plate 0 ("Before contact, 1000–1640").

### Layer toggles (drivers)
Cahokia · Climate · Iroquoian wars · French · British · American · Disease · Place names (Today)
- **Climate:** an animated **maize line**, the northern limit of reliable maize, sliding south between 1300 and 1550, plus drought shading. Keyframes need a source; until one exists, render a dashed line labeled "schematic."
- **American:** Royce cession polygons fading in by treaty year (phase 6).

### Language tree panel (linked)
- A collapsible tree: family → branch → language → dialect.
- Tapping a node highlights every nation speaking anything under that node, across all time, and dims the rest.
- **Semantic zoom.** A "level" switch recolors the map by family, branch or language.

### Data gaps panel
Lists every `needs_source` and `contested` item with its id, so the backlog is visible.

## Data model (`data/`)
- `languages.json`: `{id, name, parent, level: family|branch|language|dialect, status: living|revitalizing|unrecorded|extinct, note}`
- `nations.json`: `{id, name, language, positions:[{year,lat,lon,confidence,source,note}], alt_positions?, splits?, merges?}`
- `events.json`: `{id, year_start, year_end, title, driver, lat, lon, confidence, source, actors[], note}`
- `placenames.json`: `{name, lat, lon, language, meaning, confidence, source, note}`
- `sources.json`: `{key: {cite, url, tier}}`
- `GAPS.md`: human-readable backlog, generated by `npm run gaps`.

**Interpolation.** Positions are linear between keyframes. Before the first keyframe a nation is hidden. After the last it holds position, unless it has a merge, in which case it fuses into the target.

## Roadmap (one PR each)
1. **Scaffold.** Vite + TS, Natural Earth basemap in the atlas style, GitHub Pages deploy, validator.
2. **Time engine.** Nonlinear slider, play/pause, chapters, year readout.
3. **Nations.** Interpolated markers, trails, splits and merges, tap cards.
4. **Family fields.** KDE fields with blending, confidence weighting, and contested ghosts.
5. **Drivers.** Event pulses plus the climate maize line and the Cahokia layer.
6. **Tree panel.** Linked tree with semantic zoom.
7. **Today.** Place-name layer and present-day nations (sources required).
8. **Gaps and polish.** Data gaps panel, narration hooks (one optional audio track per chapter), and performance work on iPad.

## Out of scope (for now)
Population-accurate demography, a full Iroquoian story, and anything west of the Missouri River.
